# Resilience Context Templates

These files are **yours to fill in** and keep in your workspace as `resilience-context/`. They give the Resilience Hub power the organizational context it needs to produce accurate, non-generic assessments. Nothing here is uploaded automatically — you own these files.

| File | What it's for |
|------|---------------|
| `rto-rpo-targets.md` | Your per-workload RTO/RPO targets and chosen DR strategy tier |
| `workload-inventory.csv` | The applications/components you want assessed, with owners |
| `dependency-notes.md` | Known dependencies discovery might miss (external APIs, cross-account, on-prem) |

Fill in the placeholders (marked `<...>`), then tell the agent "use my resilience-context files".
